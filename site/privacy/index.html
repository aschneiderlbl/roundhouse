1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, business name, phone number, email address and anything you send to Hey Roundhouse. When Hey Roundhouse is part of a text thread, we receive the messages in that thread, including messages from your customers. With your permission, we also receive information from accounts you connect, such as your bank, QuickBooks and email, including customers, jobs, invoices and payments.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve Roundhouse, including scheduling, sending updates, preparing invoices and following up with customers; to process payments; to communicate with you about your account; to keep the service secure; and to comply with legal obligations. Information from your connected accounts is used only to provide the service to you.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with service providers who help us operate Roundhouse, such as text messaging, AI and payment providers, subject to confidentiality obligations.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You may also request a portable copy of your data, disconnect any connected account, or object to certain processing activities. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep it working and to understand how visitors use it. You may control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Children’s Privacy

Roundhouse is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Upon account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable period.

9. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by text, by email or through the service. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at andrew@heyroundhouse.com.
