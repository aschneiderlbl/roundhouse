About

Why I built Roundhouse

Most small businesses run on text messages. Customers text to book, reschedule, ask when you’re coming and ask about the bill. The owner answers every one, usually between jobs or late at night.

That’s back-office work, not the work they started the business to do.

I’ve worked in software since 2013, and this looked solvable. So I built Dispatch, an AI admin assistant for small businesses that sits in the thread and handles scheduling, updates, invoices and follow-ups, so owners get their evenings back.

Questions

Do my customers need an app?

No. It’s a normal group text on any phone.

Will my customers know Dispatch is AI?

Yes. When Dispatch joins a thread, Dispatch introduces itself as your AI assistant.

Does anything go out without my OK?

You decide. Routine updates can go out on their own; invoices and change orders wait for your approval.

How do I stop?

Remove Dispatch from the thread, or just tell Dispatch to stop texting you.
